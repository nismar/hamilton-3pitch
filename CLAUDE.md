# Hamilton 3-Pitch — league site sub-OS

City of Hamilton Co-ed 3 Pitch league website. Ongoing every season — not a project.

**Read `HANDOFF.md` before doing anything.** It's the full maintainer manual: weekly results workflow, data rules, deploy steps.

Hard rules (from HANDOFF.md — never break):
- Wins/losses only; never display runs or scores.
- No captain emails or phone numbers on the site.
- Internal budget/staffing notes stay off the public site.
- Every push needs a matching change-log entry in `data.js`.

- Live: https://coh-3pitch.netlify.app (auto-deploys on push to `main`)
- The weekly-scores update is Ramsin's most-hated chore — prime automation target.
